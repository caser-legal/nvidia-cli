1. "parallel_search" - tells me the exact tool
2. "Read the results" - forces me to actually process before moving on
3. "AGAIN with refined queries" - makes me iterate based on findings
4. "ONE MORE TIME" - ensures thorough coverage
5. "summarize" - gives you the answer at the end
